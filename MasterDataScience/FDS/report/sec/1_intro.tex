\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}
We have 10{,}000 training battles (exactly 50\% P1 wins) and 5{,}000 test battles.
Two findings shaped the design.
First, 17 training battles are exact duplicates (same timeline and P2 lead): we keep one copy, since twins split across CV folds leak labels, and reuse the known label for the 8 test battles that copy a training battle.
Second, P1 averages 1.69 recorded faints per battle but P2 only 0.50, while P2's Pok\'emon leave at $\leq$15\% HP without a recorded faint twice as often (0.28 vs.\ 0.12). P2 faints thus seem under-logged.
