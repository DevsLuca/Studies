\section{Evaluated Approaches}
\label{sec:approaches}

\paragraph{Feature engineering}
The \grp{cBase}{baseline} describes the state at turn 30 for each side and as a P1$-$P2 difference: Pok\'emon fainted and alive, statuses, HP at checkpoints, move usage, type matchups (Gen~2 chart), species/move bag-of-words counts and an \emph{estimated total HP} with unseen Pok\'emon at full HP, the strongest single feature.
Six groups add game knowledge.
\grp{cP2inf}{p2inf} estimates P2's unseen Pok\'emon from label-free team co-occurrence and compares P1's survivors with P2's expected pool.
\grp{cWincon}{wincon} flags boosted sweepers, Explosion, Spikes, sleep and recovery moves.
\grp{cFaint}{faint} targets the asymmetry above: Pok\'emon that left at $\leq$15\% HP and never returned count as fainted in a corrected HP total.
\grp{cItems}{items} counts P1's items; \grp{cTraj}{traj} summarises the HP-lead curve and speed control.
\grp{cFp}{fp} uses players' tendency to reuse teams: the smoothed win rate of P1's exact team and of its species set (target encoding with 5 pseudo-battles at the prior).
As it uses labels, it is computed out-of-fold, and inside CV only from that fold's training data.

\paragraph{Models}
We compared LightGBM configurations and $L_2$-regularised logistic regression.
Settings barely mattered ($<$0.004 log-loss), so we chose small randomised trees (7 leaves, 1{,}500 rounds, rate 0.02, 30\% columns, 80\% rows).
Logistic regression was best at $C{=}0.01$ but errs differently from the trees, so blending helped; the LightGBM weight was searched over $0,0.1,\dots,1$ (flat over 0.6--0.8).

\paragraph{Validation strategy}
All decisions used 5-fold stratified CV repeated twice (10 splits), with every variant evaluated on the \emph{same} splits.
Log-loss, less noisy than accuracy, was the main guide, and a change had to win most splits.
Added alone, only fp and wincon helped clearly, but together the six groups improved log-loss in 10/10 splits; removing any one except the neutral traj hurt (\cref{tab:ablation}).
