Each file has your course objectives, explanations with tables and worked examples, a glossary, exam takeaways and a standards and references table. **All the practice questions, with answers, are in one separate file: [00_knowledge_tests.md](00_knowledge_tests.md)**, grouped by module.

## How to read the markers

- **[SLIDE]** marks content taken directly from a course slide shared during the study sessions.
- **[CHECK]** marks a figure or statement from general industry practice or outside research rather than from the course slides (for example a ceiling height, a lux level, a typical efficiency or a code threshold). Verify these against the course material before the exam. If your slides give a different number, **use your slides**.
- Where the notes were written from a **lesson title only**, your slides may stress different points or use different wording. Compare the glossary and the exam takeaways in each file with your course.
- **Where a newer edition or newer figure was found, only the newer one is given**, so each figure has a single reference.
- **Every file ends with a "Standards and references used" table** naming the standards and sources behind its figures.
- Standards and codes are revised regularly. The editions named in these notes (for example ANSI/TIA-942-C, ANSI/TIA-607-E, EN 50310 with its 2026 amendment, NFPA 855 2026, NFPA 70B 2023) were current in November 2026.
